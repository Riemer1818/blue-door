## Method note — read this before citing any of it

**n = 2.** Both are academic, both Dutch, neither was a structured interview, and both were
written up from memory or chat scrollback rather than a recording. That is enough to *generate*
a hypothesis and to make one falsifiable. It is not enough to *validate* one, and it is nowhere
near enough to put a number on anything.

Two people is also a biased sample in a specific direction: academia, not industry. Every claim
here about pricing tolerance, procurement or company-lab behaviour is unevidenced.

**What we still have not talked to:** anyone in a commercial biotech, anyone who administers an
ELN rather than using one, anyone who chose and paid for one, and anyone outside NL.

## How to add one

Copy the shape of an existing file: source and date at the top, raw observations kept separate
from our interpretation, and a **Takeaways** section that says which thesis or product assumption
the conversation moves — and in which direction. Record the things that *contradict* what we
believe; those are the only observations that can change our minds.
